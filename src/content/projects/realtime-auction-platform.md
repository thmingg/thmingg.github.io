## Overview

Watch Now is Looking Watch Limited’s real-time auction product: a React admin portal for operations and a Flutter consumer app for bidding and browsing, backed by Go services on AWS/GCP.

## Highlights

- React admin portal for transactions and user notifications
- Real-time auction flows on a cross-platform Flutter client
- Deeplinks, OAuth, and Google Ad integration on mobile
- App Store and Google Play releases
- Dockerized services with PostgreSQL

## Role

Developed the web admin portal and helped maintain the auction system and Flutter consumer experience through store deployments.
