This site is my portfolio and writing space.

I’m Daniel Tsang, a web-focused software engineer. I spend most of my time building TypeScript frontends with React, Vue, and Next.js, and connecting them to Flutter apps and cloud backends on AWS and GCP.

## What’s here

- **Who Am I** — background, education, and the stack I work with
- **Projects** — selected work from recent roles (inventory platforms, AI try-on, auctions, e-commerce, clinical systems)
- **Blog** — notes on shipping product UI, modernizing frontends, and lessons from full-stack work
- **Contact** — the best ways to reach me

If you’re hiring, collaborating, or just curious about how something was built, feel free to [get in touch](/#contact).
